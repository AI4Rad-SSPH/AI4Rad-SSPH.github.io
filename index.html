Foundation Models, Multimodal Reasoning & Open Benchmarks Representation & Decision Systems

Large-scale representation learning, criteria-grounded clinical reasoning, and micro-architectural super-resolution validated across multicenter cohorts and structured radiologist reader studies.

Nature Biomedical Engineering · 2026 Skeletal Foundation Model

BoneCoT: Clinician Chain-of-Thought Whole-Body Skeleton Foundation Model

Built on BoneFM, a DINOv2-style self-supervised whole-body skeletal CT backbone, BoneCoT encodes clinician-derived task dependencies to guide step-by-step reasoning from anatomical localization and bone density changes to interpretable bone metastasis assessment across multicenter cohorts.

BoneFM Pretrained Backbone Clinician Task-Chain Multicenter Validation
Paper Code Project
Nature Communications · 2026 Emergency CT Triage

AbdomenNet: Acute Abdomen Diagnosis, Stratification & Triage on Non-Contrast CT

Pretrained via self-supervision on 103,989 non-contrast abdominal CT scans and fine-tuned on 5,816 annotated cases across 11 acute abdominal conditions and 3 risk-stratification tasks. Across 2,528 external patients from 3 cohorts, macro-AUROC reached 0.919 on emergent conditions; in a crossover reader study, AI assistance raised mean AUROC from 0.812 to 0.924 and cut median reading time by 52.5 s per case.

103,989 Pretraining CTs 3 External Cohorts Reader Crossover Study

npj Digital Medicine · 2026 Multimodal Agentic MLLM

Imaging-Grounded Multi-Agent MLLM for Acute Stroke Treatment

Emulates a multidisciplinary stroke team via specialized agents coordinating emergency triage, time-window estimation, multimodal neuroimaging interpretation, contraindication screening, and retrieval-augmented guideline reasoning. Evaluated on 1,658 suspected acute ischemic stroke cases across two centers, improving treatment recommendation accuracy from 0.636 to 0.785 (Macro-F1 0.562 → 0.768).

1,658 Two-Center Cases Contraindication Screening RAG Guideline Reasoning

npj Digital Medicine · 2026 Multicenter Report Benchmark

BrainMIND: Multicenter Benchmark & Reader Study on Brain MRI Reporting

Establishes a reproducible multicenter benchmark and blinded radiologist reader study evaluating 10 mainstream large language models on generating diagnostic impressions from free-text brain MRI report findings, systematically quantifying factual accuracy, clinical completeness, and reporting utility.

Releases the first spatially registered paired dataset of routine clinical CT and high-resolution ex-vivo micro-CT volumes of human lumbar vertebrae, bridging in-vivo clinical imaging and trabecular bone micro-architecture through an open super-resolution benchmark and community challenge.

HR-LLM-Stroke: Hierarchical Multi-Agent Emergency Stroke Decision Support

Addresses rapid multi-source clinical synthesis within narrow stroke reperfusion windows by orchestrating specialized agents for structured imaging readout, neurological feature extraction, and guideline-aligned treatment recommendation, validated across multicenter cohorts with an interactive web demo.

Hemodynamic Modeling, Quantitative Biomarkers & Clinical Studies Biophysics & Quantitative Imaging

Combining 3D vascular geometry, hemodynamic constraints, biomechanical trajectory simulation, and multi-reader clinical studies across vascular, cardiopulmonary, and musculoskeletal disorders.

IEEE J. Biomed. Health Inform. · 2026 Vascular Hemodynamics · Physics-Guided DL

FlowPredictor–Corrector: Hemodynamics-Guided Two-Step Vascular Flow Prediction

Integrates 3D vascular point-cloud representation learning with physics-based hemodynamic correction to non-invasively map 3D vascular geometry to 4D blood-flow fields, preserving physical consistency with reference CFD simulations and clinical stenosis evaluations at rapid inference speed.

Insights into Imaging · 2026 Vascular MRI Reporting · Multi-Reader Study

Criteria-Guided LLM Assistance for Carotid Plaque AHA Classification

Evaluated 10 LLMs and a 4-radiologist reader study across 433 multicenter patients with free-text carotid MRI reports, demonstrating that explicitly encoding modified-AHA staging criteria improves inter-reader agreement and enables effective deployment of a lightweight 8B model.

Academic Radiology · 2026 Cardiopulmonary CTA · Imaging Biomarker

Pulmonary Artery-to-Vein Volume Difference in Acute Pulmonary Embolism

Grounded in cardiopulmonary hemodynamics — elevated pulmonary vascular resistance and reduced left-atrial venous return — this study establishes 3D pulmonary artery-to-vein volume difference on chest CTA as an interpretable quantitative biomarker for acute pulmonary embolism risk stratification.

Quantifies vertebral artery injury risks associated with ponticulus posticus anatomical variants via 3D pedicle screw trajectory simulation across 141 cervical CTA examinations, establishing a preoperative anatomical screening workflow.
